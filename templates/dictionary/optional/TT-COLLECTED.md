# TT-COLLECTED — a screened extract of TT's own dictionary, in the template's grammar (optional layer)

Fourteen terms from the vocabulary this project actually runs on, each with the public wording its author approved
on 3 October 2026. The owner statements are the author's; the private record behind them (dates, who said what to
whom) stays private, so each `owner` line here cites that record by kind, not by pointer. Two terms the author holds
back for now are left out on purpose. Delete this file or keep it; the checker reads it only with `--with`.

- **TT** (aka: Three Tree Tenets) [Confirmed] — three tenets: Do unto others, as you wish done to yourself; Interconnectivity; Awareness plus Retention.
  - owner: the author's own statement of the three, in the first post
- **working map** [Confirmed] — what TT is: a working map towards a hoped-for future.
  - owner: the one-page summary's last line, the author's words
- **All for TT, and TT for all** (aka: the motto) [Confirmed] — TT's motto, on the first post's names line.
  - owner: the author's line on the first post
- **Language is TT** [Confirmed] — language is TT, and TT is language: a reversal, like the motto.
  - owner: the author's line, 3 Oct 2026
- **Rootward, then Sideways** (aka: rootward, sideways) [Confirmed] — work from the tip of the tree down to the root first, then sideways to every place the change reaches.
  - owner: the author's own origin story for the pair, 3 Oct 2026
- **triplet canopy** (aka: the triple) [Confirmed] — the three dictionaries, human, formal and mathematical, under one canopy.
  - owner: the author, naming the structure this template ships
- **Light** [Confirmed] — a core canopy of TT; its reason is photosynthesis: light is what the whole tree lives on.
  - owner: the author's statement, 3 Oct 2026; all canopies feed the middle
- **Symphony** [Confirmed] — taking the whole at once, never one channel alone; where passion can be heard.
  - owner: the author's listening rule, stated 23 Sep 2026
- **handoff** (aka: hand-off) [Confirmed] — a live pass of work or context from one side to the other, with the giver still there to answer.
  - owner: the author's distinction, stated when the two words were split
  - crosswalk: dropoff — the same outward act, passing something over, and a different thing underneath: the giver is present
- **dropoff** (aka: drop-off) [Confirmed] — something left at an agreed spot for the other side to pick up later, with nobody there at the same time.
  - owner: the author's distinction, stated when the two words were split
  - crosswalk: handoff — the same outward act, a different thing underneath: nobody is present
- **made date vs copy date** (aka: date kinds) [Confirmed] — a copy made today does not mean the original was made today.
  - owner: the author's rule on dating, 29 Sep 2026
- **YOUR source vs THE source** (aka: scope stamp) [Confirmed] — your earliest source is not necessarily the earliest source.
  - owner: the author's rule on origin claims, 29 Sep 2026
- **baby hydra** [Confirmed] — stale versions of one fact piling up as extra live heads when corrections are only ever added.
  - owner: the author's name for it, and the reason this dictionary keeps one active head per term
- **FOTTRP** [Confirmed] — Full Online Tree Tenets Research Pass: a research pass bounded to TT, in which curiosity met on the way may be followed.
  - owner: the author's coinage and letters, 28 Sep and 3 Oct 2026
