# HUMAN — the words people use, as they use them

One line per term. A Confirmed term carries its owner's own statement. An Unresolved term carries the reading someone
made, marked as a reading, and waits.

- **working map** (aka: the map) [Confirmed] — what this project is: a working map towards a hoped-for future.
  - owner: "a working map towards a hoped-for future" (the project's own one-page summary, last line)
- **glimmerword** [Unresolved] — reading: a word that catches the eye in a text and seems to mean more than it says; the owner has not stated it.
  - reading-by: the editor, from two uses in draft notes
- **handoff** (aka: hand-off, the handoff) [Confirmed] — a live pass of work or context from one side to the other, with the giver still there to answer.
  - owner: "a handoff is live: I'm still here when you pick it up"
  - crosswalk: dropoff — same outward act (something passed over), different thing underneath: the giver is present for a handoff and absent for a dropoff
- **dropoff** (aka: drop-off, the dropoff) [Confirmed] — something left at an agreed spot for the other side to pick up later, with nobody there at the same time.
  - owner: "a dropoff is left for later; nobody is there when it's picked up"
  - crosswalk: handoff — same outward act, different thing underneath: nobody is present for a dropoff
