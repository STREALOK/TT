# Workflow
## Observe and triage
An error is an observation; an issue is a durable problem. Record observed
time or unknown separately from recorded time, a safe signature, grouping-rule
version, attempt reference, deduplication key and bounded evidence. Expected/
unexpected and transient/persistent are independent; transient is not harmless.

Give the issue an owner, expected/observed behaviour, reproduction or an
explicit gap, first observation and earliest located source. Earliest located
does not prove true origin. Keep cause proven/suspected/unknown distinct.

Classification, lifecycle and fix state are independent. The legacy class
repaired mixes repair with knowledge; it must not promote a record to fixed
or live-verified. Score only known dimensions and state acceptance criteria
before testing.

## Test and follow through
Each fix attempt gets a trial: change/version, hypothesis, method, environment,
duration, exposures or unknown, result and source. Retain failed, blocked and
inconclusive trials. Separate synthetic, document and in-use checks.

Fixed requires explicit closure time/reason and declared acceptance scope.
A passing synthetic trial can satisfy a synthetic gate; it cannot establish
a working runtime seam. updated_at is never fixed_at. Monitoring specifies
remaining uncertainty, window and reopening trigger. Wontfix records a
decision; superseded names a successor. Neither means repaired.

On regression, add an observation, revise the issue and invalidate obsolete
screening. Retain old observations, trials and change events. Baseline comparison
covers only the supplied files and cannot reconstruct missing history.

## Rootward, then Sideways
Check the cause or nearest observable seam first, then the documented callers,
wrappers, dependencies, indexes, readers and reports. Record what was checked
and what remains unexamined. A broad scan does not substitute for a missing test.

Optional TT reading: care for affected people; map the connections; retain
evidence and uncertainty. This is a method, not a theorem or a private grade.

## Report and release
Keep issue count, error-record count, signature groups, observed occurrences,
trial count and change-event count separate. A staged collection's count is
not a count of confirmed, unique or publishable incidents.

Full open/monitoring reports carry the full lab form. Lighter closed reports
retain validation and uncertainty. Review the public projection separately.
Local report, private delivery, reviewer decision, adoption and publication
remain separate states.
