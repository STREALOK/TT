# Screening the public projection
Separate blank templates/fictional examples from the private historical
collection. Actual historical records remain staged for human review.

Write a new safe public summary. Review body and metadata: titles, filenames,
paths, links, screenshots, IDs, dates, signatures, error strings and source
references. A names mask does not remove confidences.

Exclude protected source bodies, reasoning/session transcripts, private history,
withheld application identifiers, held personal vocabulary, credentials,
unapproved sensitive material and private reverse mappings. Describe the
limits without disclosing what was withheld.

Public export refuses missing/stale/rejected screening. A recorded approval
binds revision and payload digest. Known privacy flags, unresolved redaction
or restricted/unknown rights still block export. A digest/attestation is review
evidence, not authenticated human authority or semantic safety proof.

Public output is the selected safe summary and approved excerpt, not automatic
redaction of a private record. Lighter fixed reports omit quotes and absolute
origin pointers. Private exports also need destination-specific review.

- [ ] Destination/visibility and report profile chosen
- [ ] Claims match exact test scope; unknown counts/origins retained
- [ ] Names permitted or #REDACTED#; confidences absent
- [ ] No credentials, identifying paths, reverse map or held/protected body
- [ ] Rights checked for every excerpt; no unapproved quoted works/lyrics
- [ ] Exact revision/digest reviewed; current reviewer decision recorded
- [ ] Actual rendered page/PDF and release checks run by its owner
- [ ] Commit/push/publication authorization separately established

Unticked boxes are not inferred approval.
