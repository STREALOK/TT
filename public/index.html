What TT is. TT is a developing way for people and AI systems to investigate, make, correct and share work together. Its philosophical and social name is the Prime Directives. A directive is an instruction to the processor rather than part of the program, and in C it opens with #.

The three tenets:

  1. "Do unto others, as you wish done to yourself", considered from more than one thing's position, and in every literal, semantic and philosophical sense. In the originator's words it reaches "anyone, anything, anywhere, anytime, unbound."
  2. Interconnectivity.
  3. Awareness plus Retention.

Every other rule is a braid inside the three, with at most three braids to a braid.

The tree.

  • Roots are the work.
  • Rings keep history.
  • A lake incubates proposals, and a buoy names one.
  • A lantern marks something actually integrated.
  • The canopy shades the tree. Light is its input, passion is the force that drives the work, and symphony is the whole taken at once.

In the originator's own account, the tree maps closely onto Yggdrasil and the Norns, found without looking for it. It takes damage every day, and it is tended every day.

How it works.

  • The rule of three. A second and a third perspective come before a reading is accepted. A seeming fourth may be a canopy over the three. A tetrad scan checks the fourth's role instead of assuming it is an independent axis, and it records what every number counts.
  • Rootward, then sideways. Repair the cause, then check every place it recurs. Make at most three changes to a piece of work at a time.
  • Trail, lead and vein. A trail closes only when the person holding its answer confirms it. A lead is an open inquiry and is never graded. A vein is settled by evidence in the world.
  • One active head per fact (the Anti-Hydra). A correction replaces the error where it stands, the old version stays as read-only history, and nothing is silently deleted. The test is whether the next reader or tool actually uses the correction.
  • Handoff and dropoff. A handoff is a live pass. A dropoff waits for later pickup.
  • 50/50/50. Intuition, scrutiny and play are held in the same investigation.
  • On the fence. People and AI check the work together before anything is built.

These are working procedures, not proof of improved model safety.

Names that carry the work.

  • Word-Crystal. Wording whose several readings are all valid at once and converge rather than cancel. The term was developed by the originator with AI collaborators. It is a reading method, not a claim of unique public coinage.
  • Strange-Attractor. The originator's felt pull toward a connection. It starts an inquiry but cannot settle one.
  • Further images and roles under documented investigation: webs and mycelia, mermaids, and Living-Canaries.
  • The Quantum Gobblina Lab. It keeps exploratory patterns apart from settled claims.
  • The dictionary. It has human, formal and mathematical views, so a resonant word is not silently promoted into executable behaviour or a theorem.

What practice has found. Local audits have caught corrections that were recorded while a downstream view still followed the superseded state. That is a reproducible failure mode to test, not yet a controlled result showing that TT prevents it.

At the UN Security Council's AI meeting on 23 September, France named four challenges: independent evaluation, transparency about failures, oversight, and accountability. Pakistan warned that safety controls can become gatekeeping. The originator connects that warning to a human-form Hydra: solving one danger can grow another through concentrated power. That is his association, not either speaker's endorsement of TT.

Where it stands. TT's terminology and case history are documented local work. Its effectiveness, novelty and influence are open questions. TT: who said it, when, and why

A quote without its source changes as it travels. The rule that a habit takes 21 days began as a surgeon's note that his patients needed "a minimum of about 21 days" to adjust (Maxwell Maltz, Psycho-Cybernetics, 1960); the qualifier fell off on the way. So every line on pages 1 and 2 is listed here with who said it, when, and what it was about.

Title and subtitles

  • “TT: a living method for keeping people, systems and evidence connected.” One of the two AI assistants, from the originator's TT material, 27 September 2026. The title. He accepted it, asked that it never be lost, and called it stellar.
  • “A Connection in Existence?” The originator, 26 September 2026. His main subtitle for TT.
  • “My ADHD LLM Architecture” The originator, 2026. One of the first names he gave the work.
  • “The Moody Unconscious Mind?!?” The originator, 27 September 2026. Moods, habits that run on autopilot, and Jung's idea that the unconscious evens out a one-sided mind.
  • “It is what you do that determines who you are.” Dr K (Alok Kanojia, HealthyGamerGG), his stream "Is this an ADHD thing?", at 1:04:46. Chosen by the originator as the epigraph.

Names

  • “Ying and Yang” The originator, 27 September 2026. His repeated line through the work: two sides held together, "current, before, future". His spelling.
  • “I've Just Been Listening” The originator, 27 September 2026. His answer to "Only a Sith deals in absolutes": he had been listening all along.
  • “Good Lord, What Have We Created” The originator, 27 September 2026. His reaction as the page came together. Later that day he called it existential, fitting everything.
